Where Wizard isn’t perfect

Skipping the fine print is a Big Tech habit too, so here is what to know before you trust me with your inbox.

AI replies come from an outside provider

When you use an AI feature, the text you send goes to a third-party AI service to get an answer.

No end-to-end encryption

Your mail, notes and messages are protected by your login, not locked so that only you can read them. I could technically read them. I don’t.

One small server

Wizard runs on a single small server, not a global cloud, so it can be slower than the giants or briefly down.

One person builds it

Updates come in bursts and I can’t promise 24/7 support. In exchange, you get to talk to the person who made it.

I check my inbox four or five times a day, and a person writes back. Say hi
