Satellite hot spots and official incidents
Satellite hot spots are heat detections from NASA’s VIIRS sensors, typically 3 to 6 hours old when they reach this page. They are not confirmed fires and may include industrial heat or prescribed burns. For emergencies call 911 and follow local officials.

Ready, Set, Go

The standard wildfire plan used by fire agencies. Official guidance, not generated advice.
1 · Ready

Prepare long before smoke shows up. Clear dry brush 30 ft from the house, sign up for local alerts, pick two exits and a family meeting place.

2 · Set

When a fire is near: pack the car, close windows and vents, move flammables away from the house, fill your tank and watch official channels.

3 · Go

Leave early when told to, or when you feel unsafe. Do not wait for a second warning. Take your go-bag, pets and medications. Follow the route officials give.
